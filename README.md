# CIPRB homepage draft

Extract the ZIP and open index.html. The homepage, menu, gallery and section search run locally. Navigation links are disabled for the prototype. The main header stays visible while scrolling, and the soft bottom-edge blur fades near the end of the page.

## Design references

- CIPRB (https://www.ciprb.org/) supplied the original logo, official programme photos, event imagery, partner marks and content destinations. The supplied CIPRB Terms of Reference guided the four research areas and homepage priorities.
- icddr,b (https://www.icddrb.org/) informed how research and institutional information are surfaced for a Bangladeshi public health audience.
- The George Institute for Global Health (https://www.georgeinstitute.org/) informed the organisation of research, community stories, events and support.

The homepage uses Mona Sans, a navy, blue and pale-blue palette with warm gold accents, an editorial split hero and a three-card programme section. The logo and photographs are bundled locally. Logo and programme images are from CIPRB’s official website and should be reviewed with its communications team before public release. The font license is included in assets/fonts.

The draft is a single static page. Donation calls to action are shown for the demo. The TOR’s CMS, payment gateway, sitewide repository and analytics are implementation work for the full website.

Programme banners retain their original framing and embedded text. The outline interface arrows, search icon and research-card symbols reuse Co-Gov SVG assets.

The focus grid follows the nine topics on the official CIPRB homepage. The partner grid includes the 18 entries on that homepage. WHO and RNLI have bundled logos. The other 16 marks load from their original CIPRB image URLs. Partner names remain visible if images cannot load.

## TOR coverage

- Homepage draft: organisation and management links, research and focus topics, programmes, official drowning incident mapping link, publications, news and media, events, national and international partners, careers, tenders, contact and a donation enquiry CTA.
- The homepage displays publication resource labels. The prototype search covers this homepage.
- Full-site deliverables: dynamic working-area map, infographic library, event calendar, sitemap, searchable research repository, secure payment gateway, contact automation, FAQ chatbot, analytics, Laravel CMS, SEO and content migration, accessibility testing, training and maintenance.
- The existing drowning incident mapping link is a related CIPRB resource. It does not implement the working-area map in the TOR.

Focus cards use original outline icons. Open a preview with a mouse hover or activate its button with touch or keyboard. The two programme images reuse the credited CIPRB banners. Partner logos run in two rows, with hover pausing and a static, scrollable layout for reduced-motion preferences.
